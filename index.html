AWS Builder Center

How to apply for the AWS Builder Center program

Guide by AWS Ambassador Prajwal Royston Cordiero

Open AWS Builder Center See the steps

Six steps, about five minutes

Follow these in order. You'll create your Builder Center profile, then send your details to the sheet so you're counted in the program.

  1. Open Builder Center and sign in

    Click the link above (or scan the QR code from the poster). On the "Welcome home, builder" page, click Sign in at the top right.

  2. Continue with Google

    In the sign-in window, click Continue with Google, pick any email you prefer, and continue.

    Then click BackOnce you land on the page that says "Welcome home, builder", click Back at the top of the page to return to profile setup.

  3. Set up your alias

    Your alias is your unique handle on Builder Center and can't be changed later. Use 3 to 19 characters: lowercase letters and numbers only, no capital letters or special characters. Test yours here:

    builder.aws.com/community/@
    • Be 3 to 19 characters
    • Use letters a-z, numbers 0-9

    Choose your country, then click Continue.

  4. Tell AWS you're a student

    On "Be the first to know", click Continue. On "Are you a student?", choose Yes.

    Pick your Program of study (your branch). If your branch isn't listed, choose Other. Then enter your expected graduation date in YYYY/MM format:

    Your yearGraduation date
    Third year2028/09
    Second year2029/09
    First year2029/09

  5. Copy your alias from your profile

    Click your name at the top right, then Manage profile. Your alias appears under your name, starting with @. Copy it.

  6. Add your details to the sheet

    Open the sheet and add your name, alias and email. This is the final step.
